# Hospital completion previews

These AI-edited completion concepts are based on the hospital photographs supplied by the owner. They are not photographs of completed facilities.

- `exterior-preview`: preserves the blue-glass facade and white framing; removes scaffolding and construction clutter; adds a finished canopy, paving and planting. The facade has no name lettering.
- `reception-preview`: preserves the angular white desk, fluted brown timber, cream panels and glass screen; removes temporary coverings and exposed wires; adds finished lighting. The wall has no name lettering.

PNG files are generated masters. WebP versions are used on the website. Regenerate WebP files with `php scripts/optimize-hospital-previews.php`.
